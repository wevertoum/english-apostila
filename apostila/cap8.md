# Capítulo 8 — Have

> Objetivo deste capítulo: separar os dois papéis de **have**. Um é verbo principal (“ter”). O outro é auxiliar de **perfect**. São a mesma palavra; não são a mesma máquina.

Esta é a Estrutura 4. Introdução, não um tratado de tempos perfeitos.

---

## 8.1 HAVE como verbo principal — “ter”

Aqui **have** se comporta como **need** e **work**: família dos verbos comuns.

### Presente

- I **have** a car. → Eu tenho um carro.
- I **don't have** a car. → Eu não tenho um carro.
- **Do** you **have** a car? → Você tem um carro?

He / she / it:

- She **has** a car.
- She **doesn't have** a car.
- **Does** she **have** a car?

O **-s** de have é **has**. Quando **does** entra, volta **have**:

- ❌ Does she **has** a car?
- ❌ She don't have a car.
- ✅ Does she **have** a car?
- ✅ She **doesn't have** a car.

### HAD — o passado

**Had** vale para todos os sujeitos na afirmativa.

- I **had** a car. → Eu tinha / tive um carro.
- I **didn't have** a car. → Eu não tinha / não tive um carro.
- **Did** you **have** a car? → Você tinha / teve um carro?

- She had time. / She didn't have time. / Did she have time?

**Did** já é passado. Por isso o verbo volta para **have**, não **had**:

- ❌ I didn't **had** time.
- ❌ Did you **had** a meeting?
- ✅ I didn't **have** time.
- ✅ Did you **have** a meeting?

### Futuro

- I'll **have** time tomorrow.
- I **won't have** time tomorrow.
- **Will** you **have** time tomorrow?

## 8.2 Have / has / had — tabela do verbo principal

Complemento: **time**

|  | Presente | Passado | Futuro |
|---|---|---|---|
| Afirmar (I) | I have time. | I had time. | I'll have time. |
| Negar (I) | I don't have time. | I didn't have time. | I won't have time. |
| Perguntar | Do you have time? | Did you have time? | Will you have time? |
| Afirmar (she) | She has time. | She had time. | She'll have time. |
| Negar (she) | She doesn't have time. | She didn't have time. | She won't have time. |
| Perguntar (she) | Does she have time? | Did she have time? | Will she have time? |

Se você treinar só esta tabela, **have** “ter” já está resolvido.

---

## 8.3 Have to — “ter que”

Muito comum. **Have** ainda é verbo principal; **to** + outro verbo = obrigação.

- I **have to** finish this today. → Eu tenho que terminar isso hoje.
- I **don't have to** join. → Eu não preciso entrar. *(opcional)*
- **Do** you **have to** go? → Você tem que ir?
- She **has to** review this.
- She **doesn't have to** review this.
- I **had to** leave early. → Eu tive que sair cedo.
- I **didn't have to** wait.

Isso não é modal. Por isso usa **do/does/did**:

- ❌ Have you to go?
- ✅ Do you have to go?

Lembra do Capítulo 7: *don't have to* ≠ *mustn't*.

---

## 8.4 Frases de “ter” que um adulto fala

- Do you have five minutes?
- I don't have the latest version.
- She has a meeting at three.
- We had a bug in production.
- Did you have any questions?
- I won't have access until Monday.
- Do you have a second?

Idade, de novo, **não** é have:

- ❌ I have 35 years.
- ✅ I'm 35.

*Have* de posse: *I have a car.* *Have* de idade: não.

---

## 8.5 HAVE como auxiliar — a porta do Present Perfect

Agora o outro papel. **Have** deixa de significar “ter um objeto” e vira ferramenta de tempo, como **do** e **did**.

```
sujeito + have/has + particípio + complemento
```

- I **have worked**. → Eu tenho trabalhado / já trabalhei *(depende do contexto)*.
- I **haven't worked**.
- **Have** you **worked**?

He / she / it usa **has**:

- She **has worked**.
- She **hasn't worked**.
- **Has** she **worked**?

O verbo principal vai para o **particípio** (a “terceira forma”):

| Base | Passado | Particípio |
|---|---|---|
| work | worked | worked |
| need | needed | needed |
| go | went | **gone** |
| see | saw | **seen** |
| do | did | **done** |
| send | sent | sent |
| write | wrote | **written** |
| have | had | had |

Regulares: passado e particípio são iguais (*worked / worked*). Irregulares: muitas vezes **não**.

- ❌ I have **went**.
- ❌ I have **saw** it.
- ❌ I have **wrote**.
- ✅ I have **gone**. / I've gone.  
- ✅ I have **seen** it. / I've seen it.  
- ✅ I have **written** it.

---

## 8.6 I worked × I have worked

Não transforme isto numa guerra de 40 regras. Uma distinção **pedagógica** e útil:

**I worked** — passado simples: ação **terminada**, muitas vezes com tempo claro.

- I worked from home **yesterday**.
- I sent the file **last night**.
- I didn't understand **what he said**.

**I have worked** — present perfect: liga o passado ao **agora**. Resultado, experiência, “já / ainda não / até agora”.

- I've **already** sent the file. → Eu **já** mandei o arquivo. *(o arquivo está mandado, agora)*
- I **haven't** sent it **yet**. → Eu **ainda** não mandei.
- Have you **ever** worked remotely? → Você **já** trabalhou remoto? *(experiência de vida)*
- We've worked together **for two years**. → A gente trabalha junto **há** dois anos. *(começou no passado, continua)*

Marcadores que puxam para o perfect, no começo:

| Inglês | Ideia |
|---|---|
| already | já (aconteceu) |
| yet | ainda (em pergunta/negação) |
| ever | alguma vez na vida |
| never | nunca (até agora) |
| just | acabou de |
| for | durante (duração) |
| since | desde |

Marcadores que puxam para o Past Simple:

- yesterday, last week, two days ago, in 2019, when I joined

Por isso:

- I sent it yesterday.  
- ❌ I have sent it yesterday.  
- I've already sent it.

Essa última linha é a simplificação mais útil da apostila sobre perfect. Depois você refina.

## 8.7 Contrações do auxiliar

| Completo | Fala |
|---|---|
| I have worked | **I've** worked |
| you have | **you've** |
| we have | **we've** |
| they have | **they've** |
| he has / she has | **he's / she's** worked |
| have not | **haven't** |
| has not | **hasn't** |

*He's worked* = he **has** worked.  
*He's tired* = he **is** tired.

O próximo som resolve: particípio → **has**; adjetivo/lugar → **is**.

Resposta curta:

> Have you sent it?  
> **Yes, I have.** / **No, I haven't.**

> Has she seen it?  
> **Yes, she has.** / **No, she hasn't.**

- ❌ Yes, I've.  
- ✅ Yes, I have.

---

## 8.8 Past Perfect — só um contato

```
sujeito + had + particípio
```

- I **had worked**.
- I **hadn't worked**.
- **Had** you **worked**?

Use quando uma ação no passado aconteceu **antes de outra** no passado.

- I had sent the file before she asked. → Eu já tinha mandado o arquivo antes de ela pedir.

Não precisa produzir isto o tempo todo. Reconheça **had + particípio** = “já tinha feito”.

**Had** verbo principal: *I had a car.*  
**Had** auxiliar: *I had sent it.*

Depois de *had* auxiliar, vem particípio — não *had went*.

- ❌ I had went.
- ✅ I had **gone**. / I had **sent** it.

---

## 8.9 Os dois HAVEs na mesma frase

Isso assusta e é correto:

- I **have had** this laptop for three years. → Eu tenho este laptop há três anos.  
  (auxiliar **have** + particípio de have = **had**)

- **Do** you **have** a second? → verbo principal, presente.  
- **Have** you **had** lunch? → auxiliar + particípio.

Pergunte: **depois do have vem um particípio (worked, seen, had, gone)?**  
Sim → auxiliar.  
Não, vem um substantivo (*a car, time, a meeting*) → verbo principal.

## 8.10 WH com have

Verbo principal:

- What do you have?  
- How much time does she have?

Auxiliar perfect:

- What have you done?  
- Where have you worked?  
- Have you seen my message?

- ❌ Have you saw my message?
- ✅ Have you **seen** my message?

## 8.11 Erros comuns

### Erro 1 — Did + had

- ❌ I didn't had time.
- ✅ I didn't **have** time.

### Erro 2 — Does + has

- ❌ Does she has a car?
- ✅ Does she **have** a car?

### Erro 3 — Have + passado simples irregular

- ❌ I have went / I have saw / I have did
- ✅ I have **gone** / I have **seen** / I have **done**

### Erro 4 — Perfect + yesterday

- ❌ I have sent it yesterday.
- ✅ I sent it yesterday.  
- ✅ I've already sent it.

### Erro 5 — I haven't a car (como forma principal desta apostila)

No inglês americano do dia a dia:

- ✅ I **don't have** a car.

*I haven't a car* aparece em alguns registros britânicos. Treine **don't have**.

### Erro 6 — Have to com ordem de modal

- ❌ Have you to finish this?
- ✅ Do you have to finish this?

---

## 8.12 Drill de fala (4 minutos)

**Ter**

> I have time. I don't have time. Do you have time?  
> She has time. She doesn't have time. Does she have time?  
> I had time. I didn't have time. Did you have time?  
> I'll have time. I won't have time. Will you have time?

**Have to**

> I have to go. I don't have to go. Do you have to go?

**Perfect**

> I've sent it. I haven't sent it. Have you sent it?  
> She's seen it. She hasn't seen it. Has she seen it?  
> I've already done it. I haven't done it yet.

## Resumo do capítulo

1. **Have** principal = ter. Máquina de **need**: do / does / did / will.  
2. Presente: *have / has*. Passado afirmativo: *had*. Com did: volta **have**.  
3. **Have to** = ter que. *Don't have to* = não precisa.  
4. **Have** auxiliar + **particípio** = perfect.  
5. *I worked yesterday* × *I've already worked*.  
6. ❌ have went / have saw / didn't had / does she has.  
7. I've / she's / haven't / hasn't. Sim curto: **Yes, I have.**  
8. *Had + particípio* = “já tinha feito” — reconhecer.
