# Capítulo 6 — To be

> Objetivo deste capítulo: tratar **BE** como uma família à parte. Ele **é** o auxiliar. Por isso a pergunta e a negação **não usam do / did**.

Até aqui, verbos comuns: need, work, call, send. A ferramenta era **do / does / did / will**.

**Be** (ser / estar) não pede essa ferramenta. Ele mesmo sobe no painel.

---

## 6.1 Por que BE se comporta diferente

Com **need**:

```
I            need     help.
I     don't  need     help.
Do    you    need     help?
```

Com **be**:

```
I     am            tired.
I     am     not    tired.
Am    I             tired?
```

Não entrou **do**. O **am** já faz o papel de auxiliar **e** de verbo.

Essa é a comparação mais importante do capítulo:

| Verbo comum | To be |
|---|---|
| Do you **need** help? | **Are** you tired? |
| I **don't need** help. | I **am not** tired. |
| Did you **need** help? | **Were** you tired? |
| I **didn't need** help. | I **wasn't** tired. |

- ❌ Do you are tired?
- ❌ Did you were tired?
- ❌ I don't be tired.
- ✅ Are you tired?
- ✅ Were you tired?
- ✅ I'm not tired.

Se a ação é **be**, esqueça **do** e **did**.

---

## 6.2 As formas — presente, passado, futuro

### Presente

| Sujeito | BE |
|---|---|
| I | **am** |
| he / she / it | **is** |
| you / we / they | **are** |

- I **am** tired. → Eu estou cansado.
- She **is** ready. → Ela está pronta.
- You **are** right. → Você está certo.
- They **are** on the call. → Eles estão na call.

### Passado

| Sujeito | BE |
|---|---|
| I / he / she / it | **was** |
| you / we / they | **were** |

- I **was** tired. → Eu estava cansado.
- She **was** in the meeting. → Ela estava na reunião.
- You **were** right. → Você estava certo.
- We **were** late. → A gente estava atrasado.

**You** pede **were**, mesmo no singular. Não existe *you was* no padrão que vamos treinar.

### Futuro

Aqui BE se junta ao **will** que você já conhece:

```
will + be
```

- I **will be** tired. / I'll **be** tired.
- I **won't be** tired.
- **Will** you **be** tired?

**Be** volta para a forma base depois de **will**. A mesma regra de ouro.

- ❌ I will am tired.
- ❌ She will is ready.
- ✅ I will **be** tired.
- ✅ She will **be** ready.

---

## 6.3 As nove frases-modelo

Grave este bloco. É o *need* do Capítulo 1, versão BE.

### Presente — I

- I **am** tired. → Eu estou cansado.
- I **am not** tired. → Eu não estou cansado.
- **Am** I tired? → Estou cansado?

### Passado — I

- I **was** tired.
- I **wasn't** tired.
- **Was** I tired?

### Futuro — I

- I **will be** tired. / I'll be tired.
- I **won't be** tired.
- **Will** I be tired? / **Will** you be tired? *(na conversa, pergunte ao outro)*

Agora **you** no presente e no passado (é o que mais aparece na fala):

- You are ready. / You aren't ready. / Are you ready?
- You were ready. / You weren't ready. / Were you ready?
- You'll be ready. / You won't be ready. / Will you be ready?

---

## 6.4 Afirmação, negação, pergunta

### Presente

```
AFIRMAR      sujeito + am/is/are + complemento
NEGAR        sujeito + am/is/are + NOT + complemento
PERGUNTAR    am/is/are + sujeito + complemento?
```

- She **is** on vacation.
- She **isn't** on vacation. / She's **not** on vacation.
- **Is** she on vacation?

### Passado

```
sujeito + was/were (+ not)
was/were + sujeito?
```

- They **were** in São Paulo.
- They **weren't** in São Paulo.
- **Were** they in São Paulo?

### Futuro

```
sujeito + will + be
sujeito + won't + be
Will + sujeito + be?
```

- We **will be** there at nine.
- We **won't be** there at nine.
- **Will** you **be** there at nine?

Não coloque **do** em nenhuma dessas linhas.

## 6.5 Contrações (fala real)

| Completo | Contração |
|---|---|
| I am | **I'm** |
| you are | **you're** |
| he is | **he's** |
| she is | **she's** |
| it is | **it's** |
| we are | **we're** |
| they are | **they're** |
| is not | **isn't** / 's not |
| are not | **aren't** / 're not |
| was not | **wasn't** |
| were not | **weren't** |
| will be | **'ll be** |
| will not be | **won't be** |

- I'm ready.
- She's not in the office. / She isn't in the office.
- We weren't sure.
- I'll be late.

**I am not** → **I'm not**. Não existe *I amn't* no inglês padrão.

Cuidado de orelha:

- **you're** (você é/está) ≠ **your** (seu)
- **it's** (it is) ≠ **its** (dele/dela, coisa)
- **they're** (they are) ≠ **their** (deles) ≠ **there** (lá)

### Passado

|  | Afirmar | Negar | Perguntar |
|---|---|---|---|
| I | I was tired. | I wasn't tired. | Was I tired? |
| you | You were tired. | You weren't tired. | Were you tired? |

| we | We were tired. | We weren't tired. | Were we tired? |
| they | They were tired. | They weren't tired. | Were they tired? |

|  | Afirmar | Negar | Perguntar |

## 6.7 Respostas curtas — BE se repete

> Are you ready?  
> **Yes, I am.** / **No, I'm not.**

> Is she in the meeting?  
> **Yes, she is.** / **No, she isn't.** / **No, she's not.**

> Were you there?  
> **Yes, I was.** / **No, I wasn't.**

> Were they late?  
> **Yes, they were.** / **No, they weren't.**

> Will you be there?  
> **Yes, I will.** / **No, I won't.**

No *yes* do presente, **não** contraia:

- ❌ Are you ready? Yes, I'm.
- ✅ Yes, I am.

O *no* aceita contração: **No, I'm not.**

Isso é o oposto do conforto com *don't*: aqui a peça que volta é **am/is/are/was/were**, não **do**.

---

## 6.8 WH com BE

O WH senta na frente do BE. **Sem do.**

```
WH + am/is/are/was/were + sujeito?
WH + will + sujeito + be?
```

- Where **are** you? → Onde você está?
- How **are** you? → Como vai? / Como você está?
- Who **is** she? → Quem é ela?
- When **is** the meeting? → Quando é a reunião?
- Why **were** you late? → Por que você estava atrasado?
- How **was** the call? → Como foi a call?
- Where **will** you **be**? → Onde você vai estar?

- ❌ Where do you are?
- ❌ How was you?
- ✅ Where **are** you?
- ✅ How **were** you? / How **was** the call?

## 6.9 O que BE expressa (sem lista infinita)

Para o brasileiro, *ser* e *estar* são dois verbos. Em inglês, muitas vezes é **o mesmo BE**. O contexto resolve.

**Estado / sentimento**

- I'm tired. / I'm good. / I'm not sure.

**Identidade / papel**

- I'm a developer. → Eu sou desenvolvedor.
- She's the manager.

**Local**

- We're in the other room.
- The file isn't in that folder.

**Qualidade**

- That's a good idea.
- This is not ready.

**Idade, preço, horário** — BE, não *have* nestes casos:

- I'm 35. → Eu tenho 35 anos. *(não I have 35 years)*
- The meeting is at three.

> **Simplificação:** *There is / there are* (“há / tem”) também usam BE. Reconheça: *There's a bug.* Detalhe fica para depois. O núcleo agora é **I am / you are / she is**.

---

## 6.10 BE × verbo comum — lado a lado

A mesma intenção, duas famílias.

**Presente, pergunta**

- Do you **need** a minute?  
- **Are** you **ready**?

**Presente, negação**

- I **don't need** a minute.  
- I'm **not ready**.

**Passado, pergunta**

- Did you **need** a minute?  
- **Were** you **ready**?

**Passado, negação**

- I **didn't need** a minute.  
- I **wasn't ready**.

**Futuro** — aqui as duas famílias se parecem, porque **will** entra nas duas:

- I **won't need** a minute.  
- I **won't be** ready.

- Will you **need** a minute?  
- Will you **be** ready?

O futuro é a ponte. O presente e o passado são o terreno onde o brasileiro mistura **do** com **are**.

Pergunta de checagem, sempre:

> A ação é NEED/WORK/CALL… ou é BE (ser/estar)?

Se for BE, **do** não entra.

---

## 6.11 Going to encontra BE

No Capítulo 5, *I'm going to call him* usa **am**. Agora a peça tem nome.

- I'm going to be late. → Eu vou me atrasar.
- She isn't going to be there.
- Are you going to be in the office?

Aqui há **dois** BE na prática da ideia: o **am/is/are** da estrutura *going to* e, se a ação for “estar”, o **be** depois.

- I'm going to **be** tired after that run.

Não é erro. São papéis diferentes: ferramenta + ação.

## 6.13 Erros comuns

### Erro 1 — DO + BE

- ❌ Do you are tired?
- ❌ Does she is ready?
- ❌ Did you were there?
- ✅ Are you tired?
- ✅ Is she ready?
- ✅ Were you there?

### Erro 2 — I AM AGREE

Português: “eu **estou** de acordo”. Inglês: verbo comum **agree**, não BE.

- ❌ I am agree.
- ✅ I **agree**. / I **don't agree**.

### Erro 3 — Idade com HAVE

- ❌ I have 35 years.
- ✅ I'm 35. / I'm 35 years old.

### Erro 4 — You was

- ❌ You was right.
- ✅ You **were** right.

### Erro 5 — Yes, I'm.

- ❌ Are you ready? Yes, I'm.
- ✅ Yes, I am.

### Erro 6 — Will am / will is

- ❌ I will am there.
- ✅ I'll **be** there.

### Erro 7 — To be + verbo comum no lugar errado

- ❌ I am need help.
- ✅ I need help.  
  *(need é estrutura 1; be não entra)*

## 6.14 Drill de fala (4 minutos)

### Presente

> I'm ready. I'm not ready. Am I ready?  
> You're right. You aren't right. Are you right?  
> She's late. She isn't late. Is she late?  
> It's ready. It isn't ready. Is it ready?

### Passado

> I was there. I wasn't there. Was I there?  
> You were there. You weren't there. Were you there?  
> She was sure. She wasn't sure. Was she sure?

### Futuro

> I'll be late. I won't be late. Will you be late?  
> She'll be there. She won't be there. Will she be there?

### Contraste (o ouro do capítulo)

> Do you need help? · Are you okay?  
> I don't need this. · I'm not sure.  
> Did you call her? · Were you there?  
> I'll need time. · I'll be there.

## Resumo do capítulo

1. **BE** é a Estrutura 2: o próprio verbo funciona como auxiliar.  
2. Presente: **am / is / are**. Passado: **was / were**. Futuro: **will be**.  
3. Nunca **do/does/did** com BE: ❌ *Do you are…?* ❌ *Did you were…?*  
4. Negação: *I'm not / isn't / aren't / wasn't / weren't / won't be*.  
5. Pergunta: BE vai para a frente (*Are you…? Were they…?*).  
6. Resposta curta: *Yes, I am. / No, I wasn't.* — não *Yes, I'm.*  
7. WH + BE, sem do: *Where are you? How was the call?*  
8. No futuro, BE e verbos comuns se parecem: os dois usam **will**.

---

## O que vem no Capítulo 7

A Estrutura 3: **can, could, should, would, may, might, must**. Outra família em que o modal **já é o auxiliar**, e o próximo verbo fica na base: *I can help* — nunca *I can to help* nem *She can helps*.
